## 完整範文（153 字）

The diagram illustrates the layout of a home smokery and how it operates to preserve food.

Overall, the smokery is largely built underground, with only a wooden box situated above ground level. The process involves three stages, beginning with air entering the system and ending with meat and fish being smoked.

In the first stage, air is drawn in through an open, slanted lid at ground level and travels down a deep vertical shaft until it reaches the bottom. In the second stage, the air passes into an adjacent underground chamber, which is covered by a closed lid. Here, it fuels a fire of stacked, burning logs.

In the final stage, the burning logs produce smoke, which travels through an underground pipe towards the third section. After passing over some bricks, the smoke rises above ground level into the wooden box, where the meat and fish hanging from the top are gradually cured.

## 修改紀錄

### Introduction
- 草稿 1：This provided picture illustrates a process of how a home smokery works. The structure includes one open lid, one close lid and one close box which is on the ground.
- 草稿 2：The diagram given illustrates the layout and the process of how a home smokery works
- 定稿：The diagram illustrates the layout of a home smokery and how it operates.（全文版加上 to preserve food）
- 重點：題目三塊都換掉（shows → illustrates、structure → layout、how it is used → how it operates）；layout 後要接 of；不在 Intro 列零件

### Overview
- 草稿：There are three steps in the home bakery process. The air will go through the system by an open lid. And then it goes into another room to helps the fire burn. Meanwhile, the smoke which is generated by fire goes along an underground pipe. Meat and fish are inside a wooden box, where they are grilled by the smoke.
- 問題：寫成 Body（逐步描述 + 零件），缺地上 / 地下的結構特徵
- 保留的好句構：被動 the smoke which is generated by fire、關係子句 where they are…

### Body 1
- 草稿：In step 1, the lid is opened so that air can enter the underground room. Afterwards, it enters into the second chamber where the wood is burning. Air helps the fire burn snd generates smoke.
- 問題：冠詞 ×3、enter into、it 指代不清、空氣產生煙（邏輯錯）、推論（is opened so that）

### Body 2
- 草稿：The burning woods produces smoke which travels along the pipe. Smoke rises into a wooden box where the meat and fish hanging inside are gradually cured.
- 問題：woods = 樹林、主謂一致、the pipe（第一次）、Smoke（第二次）、which / where 前加逗號
- 升級：加上 After passing over some bricks（分詞片語，補上磚塊細節）

# 書信參考範文（一般訓練組）

> 來源：2026-10-31 整理自網路文章《雅思寫作高分模板終極指南》。中文翻譯未收錄。

## 正式：投訴受損筆電

**題目**：You recently ordered a laptop computer online, but when it arrived, you found it was damaged and not functioning properly. Write a letter to the company. In your letter:
- Describe the problem with the product in detail
- Explain how this problem has affected you
- Say what you would like the company to do about it

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with a recent purchase I made through your online store. On 15th December 2025, I ordered a laptop computer (Order Number: LP-2025-4589), which arrived at my address on 20th December. Unfortunately, the product was delivered in a severely damaged condition.

Upon opening the package, I immediately noticed significant damage to the laptop. The screen has a large crack across the upper right corner, rendering approximately one-third of the display unusable. Additionally, the keyboard shows signs of impact damage, with several keys dislodged from their positions. The protective packaging appeared inadequate, with insufficient cushioning material to protect the device during transit.

This situation has caused considerable inconvenience as I urgently needed the laptop for my work commitments. I had specifically chosen express delivery to ensure the computer would arrive before an important project deadline. The damage has prevented me from completing essential work tasks, and I have been forced to use borrowed equipment, which has significantly disrupted my professional schedule.

Given these circumstances, I would appreciate a full refund of the purchase price, including the express delivery charges. I am prepared to return the damaged laptop at your expense. Alternatively, if you prefer to send a replacement, I would require assurance that it will be properly packaged and expedited to arrive within three working days.

I would be grateful if this matter could be resolved promptly. I look forward to receiving your response within seven working days.

Yours faithfully,
Chen Wei-Ting

**可學**：題目三個要點各一段，順序照題目；Dear Sir or Madam 配 Yours faithfully。

## 非正式：驚喜派對邀請

**題目**：You are organizing a surprise birthday party for a close friend. Write a letter to another mutual friend inviting them to the party. In your letter:
- Explain the occasion and why you're organizing it
- Give details about the time, date, and venue
- Suggest how they can help with the preparations

Dear Jessica,

How have you been? I hope everything's going well with you! I'm writing because I'm organizing a surprise birthday party for Amy next month, and I'd love for you to be there.

As you know, Amy's been going through a tough time lately with her job change and moving to a new apartment. I thought it would be wonderful to cheer her up with a celebration surrounded by her closest friends. Plus, she's turning 30, which is definitely worth celebrating properly!

The party is scheduled for Saturday, 8th February, starting at 7 PM. I've booked a private room at The Garden Restaurant on River Street – you know, that cozy Italian place we all went to last summer. The room can accommodate about 20 people, and I'm planning to decorate it with photos from our university days together.

I was wondering if you could help me with a couple of things. First, could you keep Amy busy that afternoon? Maybe suggest going shopping or catching a movie? That way, we can set everything up without her getting suspicious. Also, if you have time, I'd really appreciate your help making a photo collage of our best memories together.

Let me know if you can make it! I'm so excited about this, and I know Amy will be thrilled to see everyone.

Looking forward to hearing from you soon!

Best wishes,
Michael

**可學**：縮寫、Plus、you know 營造口語感；用問句和朋友說話。

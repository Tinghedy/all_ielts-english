## 折線圖：三城市月均溫

**題目**：The graph below shows the average monthly temperatures in three cities (London, Sydney, and Tokyo) over a 12-month period.

The line graph illustrates the average monthly temperatures in London, Sydney, and Tokyo throughout a year. Overall, while London and Tokyo experience relatively similar seasonal patterns with colder winters and warmer summers, Sydney demonstrates an inverse temperature trend due to its location in the Southern Hemisphere.

In terms of London's temperature pattern, the coldest month is January, with temperatures around 5°C, while the warmest period occurs in July and August, when temperatures peak at approximately 18°C. Tokyo follows a similar trajectory but with more extreme variations. The city's temperatures range from a low of 3°C in January to a high of 27°C in August, representing a substantial 24-degree difference between winter and summer.

Conversely, Sydney exhibits an opposing seasonal cycle. The Australian city experiences its warmest temperatures during January and February, reaching approximately 23°C, which coincides with the Southern Hemisphere's summer. The coldest months occur in July and August, when temperatures drop to around 12°C. This represents a more moderate temperature range compared to the other two cities.

Throughout the year, Tokyo consistently records the highest summer temperatures, while Sydney maintains the most stable climate with the smallest temperature fluctuations. London occupies a middle position, experiencing milder conditions in both summer and winter compared to Tokyo.

**可學**：Overview 只寫趨勢不寫數字；Conversely 切換到對比組；一城一組。

⚠️ **不要學**：
- due to its location in the Southern Hemisphere、coincides with the Southern Hemisphere's summer 是圖外推論，Task 1 不用寫原因
- 最後一段重複 Overview，刪掉也不影響

## 長條圖：五國家庭網路類型

**題目**：The bar chart below shows the percentage of households with different types of internet connection (Fiber optic, Broadband, Mobile internet) in five European countries (Sweden, Norway, Germany, France, UK) in 2020.

The bar chart compares the proportion of households with various internet connection types across five European nations in 2020. The data reveals significant variations in broadband adoption and connectivity patterns between countries.

Sweden emerged as the leader in high-speed fiber optic connections, with an impressive 52% of households utilizing this technology, substantially exceeding all other countries surveyed. Norway followed in second place with 38% fiber optic penetration, while Germany, France, and the UK recorded considerably lower figures of 18%, 15%, and 12% respectively.

Regarding traditional broadband connections, France topped the rankings at 65%, closely followed by the UK and Germany at 62% and 58% respectively. Sweden and Norway, despite their high fiber optic adoption, maintained moderate broadband usage at 35% and 42%.

Mobile internet connectivity presented a different pattern. The UK led this category with 48% of households relying on mobile connections, suggesting a preference for flexibility over fixed-line infrastructure. Germany and France recorded similar figures at 42% and 40%, while Sweden and Norway showed lower mobile internet adoption at 28% and 25%, likely due to their superior fixed-line infrastructure.

Overall, Sweden demonstrated the most advanced internet infrastructure with the highest fiber optic penetration, while countries like the UK and France showed greater reliance on traditional broadband and mobile solutions.

**可學**：依網路類型分三組；ranking 用語（topped the rankings、followed by、led this category）；respectively 的用法。

⚠️ **不要學**：
- suggesting a preference for flexibility、likely due to their superior fixed-line infrastructure、the most advanced internet infrastructure 都是圖外推論
- impressive 是主觀評價，描述圖表時刪掉
- Overview 放最後也可以，但建議固定放第二段，寫到一半沒時間時才不會漏掉

# Task 1 書信（General Training）

**狀態**：🔴 未開始
**所屬**：[[04-writing/README|Writing 寫作]]

## 這是什麼
一般訓練組的 Task 1（150 字 / 20 分），學術組不考。範文見 [[essays/models/letters]]。

## 必要技巧

> `[]` 內是要填的內容

### 三種語氣
| 類型 | 寫給誰 | 稱呼 | 結尾 | 署名 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 正式 | 陌生人、公司、機構 | Dear Sir or Madam, | Yours faithfully, | 全名 |
| 正式（知道姓名） | 知道姓名的負責人 | Dear Mr / Ms [姓], | Yours sincerely, | 全名 |
| 半正式 | 房東、鄰居、服務提供者 | Dear Mr / Ms [姓], | Kind regards, / Best regards, | 全名 |
| 非正式 | 親友 | Dear [名], / Hi [名], | Best wishes, / Love, | 名字 |

### 段落
開頭（寫信目的）→ 主體 2–3 段（每段回應一個題目要點）→ 結尾（期待的行動）→ 署名。

### 正式
開頭：
- 投訴：I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with [product / service].
- 申請：I am writing to apply for the position of [job] as advertised in [source] on [date].
- 詢問：I am writing to inquire about [topic].

主體：
- I would like to draw your attention to…
- The primary reason for my dissatisfaction is…
- The problems I encountered include…
- I would be grateful if you could provide information regarding…
- Additionally, I would like to know whether…
- I would like to request a full refund and an official apology for…

結尾：
- I look forward to receiving your response at your earliest convenience.
- I would appreciate it if this matter could be resolved promptly.
- I would be grateful if you could consider my application favourably.

### 半正式
開頭：
- I am writing with regard to…
- I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing concerning…
- I am getting in touch about…

主體：
- I would like to discuss the possibility of…
- I was wondering if it would be possible to…
- I am concerned about… and would appreciate your attention to this matter.

結尾：
- Thank you for your attention to this matter.
- I look forward to hearing from you soon.
- I would be grateful for your cooperation in this matter.

情境句：
- 致房東：I am writing regarding some maintenance issues in the apartment I am currently renting at [address]. / I have noticed that… and this has caused considerable inconvenience because… / I would appreciate it if these issues could be addressed within the next [time].
- 致鄰居：I am writing to inform you about [event] that will take place at my residence on [date]. / The event will start at [time] and is expected to finish by [time]. / I apologise in advance for any inconvenience this might cause.

### 非正式
開頭：
- How are you? I haven't heard from you in ages!
- I hope you're doing well. I'm writing to…
- Great to hear from you last week! I thought I'd write back to…

主體：
- I've been meaning to tell you about…
- You won't believe what happened last week…
- I was wondering if you'd like to…

結尾：
- Looking forward to seeing you soon!
- Let me know what you think.
- Hope to hear from you soon!
- Give my regards to your family.

情境句：
- 分享近況：I've got some exciting news to share – [news]! / What have you been up to lately?
- 感謝：I just wanted to write and say a massive thank you for [help / gift]. / Your help really meant a lot to me because… / I hope I can return the favour someday!
- 邀請：I'm writing to invite you to [event] on [date] at [place]. / It would be great if you could make it. / Let me know if you can come.

## 常見錯誤
- 同一封信混用不同正式程度的語氣
- 稱呼和結尾不配對（Dear Sir or Madam 配 Yours sincerely）
- 漏掉結構元素，或沒回應題目列的每個要點
- 正式信用縮寫（I'm、don't）

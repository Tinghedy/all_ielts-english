| 圖表 | 核心任務 | 時態 |
|---|---|---|
| 折線圖 | 描述隨時間的趨勢 | 依圖上年份，過去多用過去式 |
| 長條圖 | 比較與排序 | 同上 |
| 圓餅圖 | 表達佔比 | 同上 |

## 必要技巧

> `[]` 內是要填的內容

### 折線圖
- [Item] rose / fell from [X] to [Y] between [year] and [year].
- There was a [sharp / gradual] [increase / decline] in [item] from [year] to [year].
- [Item] experienced a [steady / sharp] [rise / fall], reaching a peak / trough of [X] in [year].

### 長條圖
比較：
- [A] was significantly / slightly higher than [B], with figures of [X] and [Y] respectively.
- While [A] accounted for [X], [B] reached only [Y].
- The gap between [A] and [B] was [X].

排序：
- [A] topped the list with [X], followed by [B] at [Y].
- At the bottom of the ranking was [C], which recorded the lowest figure of [Z].
- Among all categories, [A] was the most significant, accounting for [X].

### 圓餅圖
佔比：
- [Item] accounted for / made up / represented [X] of the total.
- [Item] took up / comprised [X] of [the whole].
- The proportion of [item] stood at [X].

比大小：
- [A] was the largest category, representing [X], while [B] was the smallest segment at just [Y].
- [A] and [B] together accounted for over half / two-thirds of the total.
- [A] was twice as large as / three times the size of [B].

### 表格
先篩選：最高 / 最低值、最大變化、明顯趨勢、異常值。不要全部描述。

橫向比較（同時間、不同類別）：
- In [year], [A] recorded a figure of [X], which was significantly higher than [B] at [Y].
- Among all categories in [year], [A] stood out with the highest value of [X].

縱向比較（同類別、不同時間）：
- The figure for [item] increased from [X] in [year] to [Y] in [year], representing a [Z] rise.
- Over the period from [year] to [year], [item] experienced the most significant growth, with figures changing from [X] to [Y].

### 流程圖
| 階段 | 順序詞 |
|---|---|
| 開始 | Initially, First, To begin with, In the first stage |
| 中間 | Next, Then, After that, Subsequently, Following this |
| 結束 | Finally, Eventually, Ultimately, In the final stage |

- 人為製造用被動：The raw materials are first collected. / Once processed, the products are packaged.
- 自然過程用主動：Water evaporates from the ocean surface. / The vapour then rises into the atmosphere.

### 地圖
- Between [year] and [year], the [old feature] was replaced by / transformed into [new feature].
- While in [year] there was a [feature] in the [location], by [year] it had been converted into [new feature].
- The most significant change was the construction of [feature] in the [location], where previously there had been [old feature].

## 常見錯誤
- 把所有數字都寫進去，沒有篩選
- 長條 / 圓餅題只列數字，沒有比較
- 流程圖用 Meanwhile 表示先後（Meanwhile = 同時）
- 地圖 by + 年份該用過去完成式（had been expanded）卻用過去式
- 想解釋數據的原因（Task 1 只描述，不推論）

# 連貫與銜接

**狀態**：🔴 未開始
**所屬**：[[04-writing/README|Writing 寫作]]

## 這是什麼
Coherence & Cohesion 佔 25%。

## 必要技巧
- 段落間用 topic sentence 串接
- 避免連接詞過度堆疊
- 代名詞與指涉要清楚
- 邏輯已經清楚時，不必再加連接詞

### 連接詞（依功能）
| 功能 | 用語 |
|---|---|
| 添加 | furthermore, in addition, moreover, additionally |
| 相似 | similarly, likewise |
| 對比 | however, nevertheless, nonetheless, in contrast, conversely |
| 讓步 | although, even though, despite this, admittedly |
| 原因 | because, due to, owing to, as a result of |
| 結果 | therefore, consequently, thus, hence, accordingly |
| 舉例 | for instance, for example, to illustrate, a case in point is, namely |
| 總結 | in conclusion, to summarise, in summary, overall |

### 同樣意思，文法不同
| 後面接 | 用語 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 子句 | because, although, even though | Prices rose because demand increased. |
| 名詞 | due to, owing to, as a result of, despite | Prices rose due to higher demand. |
| 獨立成句，前面是句號 | however, therefore, moreover | Demand increased. Therefore, prices rose. |

## 常見錯誤
- 每句開頭都放連接詞，讀起來生硬
- due to / despite 後面接完整子句
- 用 however 接兩個子句（要改成句號或分號）
- Firstly… Secondly… Thirdly… 全篇照排

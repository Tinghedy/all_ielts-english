# 地圖方位與變化（Task 1）

> 來源：2026-10-31 整理自網路文章《雅思寫作高分模板終極指南》→ [[04-writing/skills/task-1-chart-types]]
> 頻率指在 Task 1 地圖題中的常用程度，由整理時判斷；例句為整理時自擬

## 方位

| 單字 / 搭配詞 | 中文 | 頻率 | 例句 | 來源技能 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| north / south / east / west | 北 / 南 / 東 / 西 | 高 | in the north of the town | writing |
| northeast / southeast / northwest / southwest | 東北 / 東南 / 西北 / 西南 | 高 | in the southeast | writing |
| adjacent to | 鄰近 | 高 | adjacent to the park | writing |
| opposite | 在…對面 | 高 | opposite the school | writing |
| alongside | 沿著 | 中 | alongside the river | writing |
| between | 在…之間 | 高 | between the road and the lake | writing |
| surrounding | 環繞 | 中 | the surrounding farmland | writing |
| in the centre of | 在…中心 | 高 | in the centre of the town | writing |

## 變化

| 單字 / 搭配詞 | 中文 | 頻率 | 例句 | 來源技能 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| build / construct | 建造 | 高 | A road was constructed. | writing |
| develop / establish | 開發 / 設立 | 高 | An industrial zone was established. | writing |
| demolish | 拆除 | 高 | The factory was demolished. | writing |
| remove / clear | 移除 / 清除 | 高 | The trees were cleared. | writing |
| replace（A was replaced by B） | 取代 | 高 | The farm was replaced by housing. | writing |
| convert into / transform into | 改建為 / 轉變為 | 高 | It was converted into a museum. | writing |
| expand / extend | 擴建 / 延伸 | 高 | The harbour was expanded. | writing |
| enlarge / widen | 擴大 / 拓寬 | 中 | The road was widened. | writing |
| reduce / shrink / narrow | 縮小 / 收縮 / 變窄 | 中 | The park was reduced in size. | writing |

## 易混淆

| 對比 | 差別 |
|---|---|
| A was replaced by B | 舊的 A 被新的 B 取代，不要寫反 |
| in the north of / to the north of | 在範圍內的北邊 / 在範圍外的北方 |
| center / centre、harbor / harbour | 美式 / 英式，全篇統一 |

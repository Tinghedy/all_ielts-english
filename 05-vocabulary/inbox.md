  - 📝 Regular exercise helps people burn more calories and maintain a healthy weight. (規律運動有助於人們燃燒更多卡路里並維持健康的體重。)
- start my graduate school
  - 💡 graduate school (n.) 研究所；通常搭配動詞 start, enter 或 apply for。
  - 📝 Many students feel anxious before they start their graduate school journey. (許多學生在開始他們的研究所歷程之前都會感到焦慮。)
- cannot fully substitute
  - 💡 substitute (v. / n.) 代替、替代；常搭配 전치사 with 或動詞 as，如 substitute A with B。
  - 📝 Online courses cannot fully substitute traditional classroom teaching. (線上課程無法完全取代傳統的課堂教學。)
- interaction that takes place in a real classroom
  - 💡 take place (v. 片語) 發生、舉行（指事件按計畫發生，不用於被動語態）。
  - 📝 Face-to-face interaction that takes place in a real classroom is crucial for effective language learning. (在真實教室中發生的面對面互動對於有效的語言學習至關重要。)
- admittedly
  - 💡 adverb 誠然、無可否認地（常用於句首，用來承認某個事實或讓步）
  - 📝 Admittedly, renewable energy sources can be intermittent and difficult to store. (誠然，再生能源可能具有間歇性且難以儲存。)
- nevertheless
  - 💡 adv. 然而，儘管如此（表轉折，常置於句首或句中，連接兩個相對的子句）
  - 📝 The initial research was inconclusive; nevertheless, the team decided to proceed with the experiment. (初步研究結果並不確定；然而，團隊仍決定繼續進行實驗。)
- tend to
  - 💡 傾向於，動詞片語，後接原形動詞（常用於表達習慣或普遍現象）
  - 📝 University students tend to spend more time studying in the library during exam periods. (大學生在考試期間往往花更多時間在圖書館學習。)
- particularly valuable for
  - 💡 valuable (adj.) 有價值的、有用的；be valuable for... 表示「對……很有價值／很有幫助」，常用於表達事物的正面效益。
  - 📝 Acquiring practical skills is particularly valuable for graduates entering a competitive job market. (獲得實用技能對於進入競爭激烈就業市場的畢業生來說特別有價值。)
- respond to confusion
  - 💡 v. 回應、對...做出反應（phr. respond to，常搭配 sth./sb. 使用）
  - 📝 The government needs to respond to public confusion regarding the new tax policy. (政府需要針對大眾對新稅收政策的混淆做出回應。)
- self-discipline
  - 💡 n. 自律，自我約束（常用搭配詞如：have self-discipline, practice self-discipline）
  - 📝 Developing strong self-discipline is essential for achieving long-term career goals. (培養強大的自律能力對於實現長期職涯目標至關重要。)
- supervision
  - 💡 n. 監督、管理；指對人員、工作或過程進行指導與查看，常用搭配詞有 under the supervision of...
  - 📝 The research project was conducted under the close supervision of a senior professor. (這項研究計畫是在一位資深教授的密切監督下進行的。)
- conflict
  - 💡 n. 衝突、爭執（常與 between, with 搭配使用）；v. 衝突、抵觸（常與 with 搭配使用）
  - 📝 Remote work can sometimes create conflict between professional responsibilities and personal life. (遠距工作有時會在職業責任與個人生活之間產生衝突。)
- drop out of
  - 💡 phrv. 退學、休學（後接學校等機構名，常用於談論教育議題）
  - 📝 Some students decide to drop out of university to pursue entrepreneurial ventures. (有些大學生決定休學去追求創業。)
- In my past experience
  - 💡 experience (n.) 經驗、經歷；常用片語 in my past experience 表示「在我過去的經驗中」。
  - 📝 In my past experience, working in a multicultural team enhances communication skills. (在我過去的經驗中，在多元文化團隊中工作能增進溝通技巧。)
- rote
  - 💡 n. / adj. 死記硬背（通常作形容詞用，如 rote learning）

- continuous assessment
  - 💡 n. 持續評量、平時考核（指透過日常作業、專案等而非單一期末考來評估學生表現）
  - 📝 Many educators believe that continuous assessment reduces students' exam stress. (許多教育工作者認為，持續評量能減輕學生的考試壓力。)
- exam pressure
  - 💡 n. 考試壓力，指面臨考試時產生的緊張與焦慮感（常見搭配詞如 cope with exam pressure）
  - 📝 Many high school students struggle to cope with exam pressure. (許多高中生努力應付考試壓力。)
- academic subject

  - 📝 Students should explore a wide range of academic subjects before choosing their university major. (學生在選擇大學主修之前，應該探索廣泛的學術科目。)
- equally crucial
  - 💡 equally crucial (adv. + adj.) 同等重要的，常用於強調兩個或多個因素具備相同的關鍵性。搭配詞：equally crucial to / for。
  - 📝 Developing renewable energy sources is equally crucial for a sustainable future. (發展可再生能源對於永續未來同樣至關重要。)
- participate in a wide range of activities
  - 💡 phr. 參與各式各樣的活動（動詞片語，常搭配 a wide range of 表示範圍廣泛）

- uncover talents

  - 📝 Effective managers know how to uncover talents in their employees to build a stronger team. (優秀的管理者懂得如何發掘員工的才能來打造更強大的團隊。)

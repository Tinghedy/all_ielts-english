# 寫作同義替換

> 來源：2026-10-31 整理自網路文章《雅思寫作高分模板終極指南》→ [[04-writing/skills/lexical-range]]
> 頻率指在 Task 2 的適用程度，由整理時判斷；例句為整理時自擬。標「低」的字原文有列，但語域太文或太罕見，認得就好
> 連接詞的替換與文法差異見 [[04-writing/skills/cohesion]]

## 動詞

| 單字 / 搭配詞 | 中文 | 頻率 | 例句 | 來源技能 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| increase → surge | 激增 | 中 | Demand has surged in recent years. | writing |
| increase → escalate | 升高（多指問題、衝突） | 中 | Tensions have escalated. | writing |
| increase → intensify | 加劇 | 中 | Competition has intensified. | writing |
| increase → soar | 飆升 | 中 | House prices have soared. | writing |
| increase → proliferate | 數量激增、擴散 | 低 | Fake news has proliferated online. | writing |
| decrease → decline | 下降 | 高 | Birth rates have declined. | writing |
| decrease → diminish | 減弱 | 中 | Its influence has diminished. | writing |
| decrease → dwindle | 逐漸減少 | 低 | Resources are dwindling. | writing |
| decrease → recede | 消退 | 低 | The threat has receded. | writing |
| show → demonstrate | 證明、顯示 | 高 | This demonstrates that… | writing |
| show → illustrate | 說明、呈現 | 高 | This example illustrates… | writing |
| show → indicate | 顯示、指出 | 高 | The data indicate that… | writing |
| show → depict | 描繪 | 中 | The chart depicts… | writing |
| show → portray | 刻畫（多指人或形象） | 低 | Films portray teachers as heroes. | writing |
| think → believe | 認為 | 高 | I believe that… | writing |
| think → maintain / contend | 主張 | 中 | Some contend that… | writing |
| think → assert | 斷言 | 中 | Critics assert that… | writing |
| think → consider（+ 受詞 + 形容詞） | 認為 | 中 | Many consider it unfair. | writing |
| help → facilitate（+ 事，不接人） | 促進 | 高 | Technology facilitates learning. | writing |
| help → contribute to（+ 名詞） | 有助於 | 高 | Exercise contributes to good health. | writing |
| help → foster / promote | 培養 / 促進 | 高 | Schools foster creativity. | writing |
| help → enhance | 提升 | 高 | Reading enhances vocabulary. | writing |

## 易混淆

| 對比 | 差別 |
|---|---|
| facilitate / help | facilitate 後面接事情，不能接人 |
| contribute to / help | to 是介系詞，接名詞或 V-ing |
| detrimental / bad | detrimental 要接 to |
| insignificant / small | 重點是不重要，不只是小 |
